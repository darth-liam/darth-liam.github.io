Laser Cutting | Los Angeles, CA (2023-Present)

I picked up laser cutting in 2023 in Los Angeles as another outlet for hands-on fabrication alongside my work with Flagship Combat Robotics. I mostly use it to make gifts and nerdy display pieces, like layered arrangements of video game maps and engraved concept art of characters I like.

It's become a fast way for me to turn a design idea into a finished physical piece in an afternoon, whether that's a birthday gift or something for my own room.

[Quoted from the Creality Falcon Website]

The Falcon2 Pro is an enclosed diode laser engraver and cutter, available in 22W, 40W, and 60W configurations, built for both detailed engraving and clean, single-pass cuts.

The 60W version can cut through up to 22mm of wood, 33mm of black acrylic, or 0.2mm stainless steel in a single pass, with a CoreXY design supporting engraving speeds up to 600mm/s over a 400x400mm work area. A built-in camera, air assist, and a triple monitoring system for airflow, lens cleanliness, and flame status keep longer batch jobs safe and precise.

That combination of power and safety features makes it practical to run detailed, multi-hour engraving jobs without having to babysit the machine the whole time.
