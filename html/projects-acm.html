ACM Game Studio | Los Angeles, CA (2018-2019)

I served as the Economy/Progression Lead for Project Delve, a Hades-inspired roguelike with diverse biomes and increasingly difficult enemy spawns. My role focused on ensuring the game's progression system felt balanced and fair, scaling the difficulty at a rate that didn't negate the player's upgrades while still challenging their skills in later worlds. The home base consisted of both cosmetic and utilitarian upgrades, strengthening the motivation for players to return to previous levels for the resources they need to progress further.

I served as the Gameplay Mechanics Lead for the focus of two Game Jam projects: Phemie's Fable and Off My Paw-Perty, where I was responsible for designing and implementing core gameplay systems for a 2D platformer game. This included creating physics-based movement mechanics, enemy AI behaviors, and level progression systems. I also mentored junior developers and collaborated with artists to ensure seamless integration of gameplay features with visual assets.

Our mission is to teach skills revolving around video game development such as computer science, game design and art in order to help usher students into the game development industry.

Each year, Studio splits into teams of students who work through the winter and spring quarters to put together an indie game. Students can apply to be team leads to pitch their idea to the club and have people join them to bring their game concept into reality!
