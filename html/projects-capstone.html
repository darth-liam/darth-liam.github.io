UCLA Senior Capstone: Digital Leveler and Visualizer | Los Angeles, CA (Jan 2025-Mar 2025)

For my UCLA senior capstone, I built an embedded digital level from the ground up using an FPGA, a gyroscope, C, and Verilog. The system reads rotational data from the gyroscope, calibrates and integrates the measurements into an angle, converts that angle into a surface slope, and renders the result in real time on a VGA display.

One of the biggest engineering challenges was getting the software and hardware sides to communicate reliably through shared BRAM. I used Vivado's Integrated Logic Analyzer to debug data-pipeline failures and worked through sensor scaling, timing, and drift issues to get consistent readings.

The project also included a physical CAD assembly for the leveling mechanism, connecting the FPGA system to a practical mechanical application rather than leaving it as a bench-top demo.

It was a good capstone for me specifically because it forced the software, digital logic, and mechanical sides of my background to work together on one shared timing budget instead of staying in three separate projects.
