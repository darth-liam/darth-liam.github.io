BruinSwap | Los Angeles, CA (2022-2023)

BruinSwap is a peer-to-peer marketplace I helped build as a Full-Stack Web Developer, letting UCLA students buy, sell, and trade items with each other without paying any platform fees. I worked on secure SQL-based authentication and the transaction infrastructure that kept user data and listings consistent across the platform.

I also helped maintain a shared codebase across a team of six developers by setting up structured Git workflows, which cut our merge conflicts by about 30%, and worked on the Node.js landing page and peer-to-peer messaging board that let students coordinate trades directly, keeping the platform at 98% uptime.

BruinSwap grew out of a simple problem: UCLA students already trade textbooks, dorm furniture, and other items constantly, usually through scattered Facebook groups and GroupMe chats. We built it as a dedicated marketplace where students could list items, message each other, and complete trades in one place, without the fees or clutter of those informal channels.

The project ended up supporting over 200 UCLA students during its active use, and taught me as much about working on a shared codebase with five other developers as it did about the actual web stack — from setting up Git workflows that didn't slow the team down, to designing an auth system I actually trusted.
