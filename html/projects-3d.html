3D Printing | Phoenix, AZ & Los Angeles, CA (2014-Present)

I've been 3D printing since 2014, starting in Phoenix, AZ and continuing through my time in Los Angeles. What began as a hobby of printing replicas of video game items grew into a practical rapid-prototyping tool I now rely on for my larger engineering projects, including chassis components and jigs for Flagship Combat Robotics.

My current printer is a Bambu Lab X1 Carbon with the Automatic Material System (AMS), which lets me combine multiple colors and materials in a single print without manually swapping filament.

[Quoted from the Bambu Lab Website]

The Bambu Lab X1 series are state-of-the-art desktop 3D printers built with a CoreXY high-speed design and multi-color capabilities, engineered for makers at any skill level.

The X1 Carbon reaches print speeds of up to 500mm/s while maintaining precision, using AI vision and LiDAR-based sensors to automatically calibrate and monitor each print. Paired with the AMS, it can hold up to four spools of filament and switch between them mid-print, enabling detailed multi-color and multi-material models.

That combination of speed and reliability is what makes the X1 Carbon as useful for rapid-prototyping structural parts as it is for printing highly detailed display pieces.
