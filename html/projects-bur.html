Bruin Underwater Robotics | Los Angeles, CA (2023-2024)

I worked as an Autonomous Systems Software Developer for Bruin Underwater Robotics (BUR), where I developed autonomy control software for an underwater robot, including real-time feedback control loops for depth. I improved closed-loop stability by reducing oscillations 40% through PID tuning and dynamic response testing.

I also generated over 2,000 simulated sensor frames and more than 5 hours of synthetic mission data using Unity's simulation tools, then used that data to run simulation-driven reliability testing across 8 mission scenarios, increasing autonomous control stability by 45%. On the hardware side, I integrated the control software with thrusters, depth sensors, and embedded hardware using ROS and C++ to support autonomous underwater pathing missions.

[Quoted from the Bruin Underwater Robotics Website]

Bruin Underwater Robotics (BUR) is a technical project dedicated to creating robots capable of operating in and exploring underwater environments.

BUR is a student-led technical initiative that challenges members to solve complex engineering problems through collaborative design, fabrication, and testing of underwater autonomous vehicles, drawing on expertise from fluid dynamics, heat transfer, mechanisms, and circuit design.

That mix of software and hardware is what made the team a good fit for me: designing a stable control loop only matters if it can survive being integrated onto a real underwater vehicle.
